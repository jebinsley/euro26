Must-do:

· Faraglioni rocks — iconic sea stacks, best seen from a boat or Via Tragara walk
· Via Krupp — dramatic zig-zag path carved into the cliff face (if open)

Positano & Amalfi Coast — Dining & Drinks
La Sponda · Ristorante Max · Praiano wander · Music on the Rocks
›
Book Ahead — Special Occasion:
· La Sponda (Le Sirenuse hotel) — 400 candles every night, one of the most romantic restaurants on the Amalfi Coast. Jacket suggested. Book well ahead.
· Ristorante Max — Local favourite in Positano. Excellent pasta, relaxed atmosphere. Good walk-in odds on weeknights.

Wander & Discover:
· Via dei Mulini — Positano's main walking street down to the beach. Wind down it and stop wherever catches your eye — a dozen good spots along the way.
· Praiano — 10 min drive from Positano, far fewer tourists, excellent small restaurants right on the water. Ask the Airbnb host for their favourite.
· Amalfi town — Walk past the cathedral and up into the back streets. Trattoria Il Mulino up in the hills is excellent and well away from the waterfront tourist traps.

Drinks:
· Music on the Rocks — Built into a cave at the beach, great for a late-night cocktail.
· La Tagliata (Montepertuso) — High above Positano, terrace views worth the drive alone. More a restaurant but perfect for sunset drinks.
Book Now La Sponda — September is still peak season, tables go fast
